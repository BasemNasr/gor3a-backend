Gor3a (جرعة) — Your Smart Medication Companion & Prescription Scanner

Have trouble reading difficult handwritten prescriptions? Looking for affordable alternatives with the same active ingredients, or need a reliable daily reminder for your medication schedule?
Gor3a combines multimodal clinical AI vision with a comprehensive Egyptian pharmaceutical database to make your health journey clearer, safer, and simpler.

Key Features:

📸 1. AI Prescription Scanner (Clinical Vision):
• Snap a clear photo of any handwritten prescription with your camera.
• Smart AI recognizes medication brand names, strengths, forms (tablets, syrups, drops, injections), and Arabic dosage instructions.
• Clarifies complex medical shorthand into clear, actionable Arabic and English steps.

🔍 2. Comprehensive Medicine Directory & Generics:
• Search across 25,000+ Egyptian pharmaceuticals by brand or generic name.
• Discover identical generic alternatives (Exact Generics) with the same active ingredient and strength to save on healthcare costs or when medicines are out of stock.
• Review indications, mechanism of action, precautions, and manufacturer details.

💰 3. Guideline Prices & Strip Breakdown:
• Check indicative retail pack prices and automatic per-strip price calculations to manage your family's medication budget.

⏰ 4. Smart Medication Reminders & Alarms:
• Set up customized daily medication alarms tailored to your doctor's instructions (before/after meals, morning/evening).
• Never miss a dose with clear, timely local alerts.

☁️ 5. Secure Prescription History:
• Organize and backup your past prescriptions in your private medical history.
• Easily access past treatments to share with your consulting doctor during follow-ups.

🔒 Privacy & Security:
Your privacy is our utmost priority. All prescription data and personal notes are transmitted and stored using industry-standard encryption protocols.
