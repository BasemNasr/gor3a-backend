Dear Google Play Policy Review Team,

We have clearly cited and provided the direct, functional official government URL from which public medication reference information is sourced:
Official Source: https://www.edaegypt.gov.eg/ (Egyptian Drug Authority)

3. App Content Government Declaration:
In the Play Console under "App Content -> Government Apps", we have confirmed the selection that the app is NOT developed on behalf of or affiliated with a government entity.

4. Medical Safety & Informational Purpose:
We have reinforced that the application's AI OCR and medication reminder features are solely for personal organizational and informational assistance, and do not constitute medical diagnosis or prescribe treatments.

We have updated the Full Description in our Google Play Store Listing accordingly and submitted the changes for your review. We kindly request the team to re-review and approve the update.
